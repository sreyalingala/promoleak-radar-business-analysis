# Stakeholder Register

**Project:** PromoLeak Radar  
**Maintained by:** BA  
**Review cadence:** After big workshops or if reporting lines shift.

| ID | Name / group | Role | Interest | Influence | Notes (initial) | How to work with them |
|----|----------------|------|----------|-----------|-----------------|------------------------|
| S01 | VP Finance | Sponsor / approver | Credible leakage story, margin | High | Will slow-roll anything that looks like a science project | 1-pagers; lead with numbers; don’t bury assumptions |
| S02 | Director, Growth / Promotions | Promo policy | Conversion, CAC | High | Pushes back on hard caps unless the pain is proved on *their* campaigns | Show category-level pain first; pilot one bad segment if needed |
| S03 | E-commerce Product | Checkout, cart, UX | Fewer fires, predictable releases | Medium | Waiting to see if this becomes “another fraud project” | Workshop on flows + NFRs; keep scope visible |
| S04 | Fraud / Risk (if split out) | Investigations | Case tool, audit trail | Med–High | Fine helping if Legal has already blessed language | Walkthrough real escalations, not hypotheticals |
| S05 | Customer Service | Tickets | Scripts, fairness | Medium | Tired of “special” handling per agent | Loop in before customer-visible rule changes |
| S06 | Data / BI | Marts, dashboards | Time; hates one-off pulls | Medium | Will say no if we don’t prioritize | Data reqs early; smallest useful dashboard first |
| S07 | Legal / Compliance | Terms, privacy | No nasty surprises | Medium | Blocks vague clawback wording | Before UAT on anything customer-facing |
| S08 | Engineering | OMS, promo, identity | What’s feasible | High | Wants written reqs, not hallway scope | RTM + spikes where rules touch legacy |
| S09 | Merchandising / Category | SKU economics | Hero SKUs not given away | Medium | Cares about loss leaders | Margin floors in business rules doc — they need to own the % |
