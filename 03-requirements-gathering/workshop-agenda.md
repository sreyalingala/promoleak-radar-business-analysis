# Workshop Agenda — Promo Mechanics & Leakage (90 minutes)

**Objective:** Align on as-is pain points, agree on priority abuse categories, and capture decisions for the BRD slice on rules and monitoring.

## Pre-read (send 48h ahead)

- Problem statement (1 page)
- Draft list of promo types in scope

## Attendees (target)

Finance (1), Growth (1–2), Product (1), Engineering (1), Data (1), CS (optional), Legal optional for second session if policy-heavy.

## Agenda

| Time | Topic | Facilitator | Output |
|------|-------|-------------|--------|
| 0:00–0:10 | Context and goals for the session | BA | Shared understanding |
| 0:10–0:35 | As-is walk: one happy path + two failure paths | Product / Growth | Whiteboard flows |
| 0:35–0:55 | Abuse categories — define in business language | BA | Draft glossary |
| 0:55–1:15 | Prioritization: impact vs. feasibility matrix | Group | Top 3 themes for MVP |
| 1:15–1:25 | Decisions / parking lot / owners | BA | Action list |
| 1:25–1:30 | Next steps and follow-up sessions | BA | Calendar holds |

## Ground rules

- “Customer always cheating” vs. “system allowed it” — separate blame from system behavior.
- Parking lot for legal-heavy items if counsel not in room.

## Materials

- Screen share of sample order if available (anonymized).
- Sticky notes or digital board.

## Follow-up template

Each action: **What** / **Owner** / **Due** / **Linked requirement ID** (once RTM exists).
