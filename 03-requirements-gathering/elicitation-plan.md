# Elicitation Plan

**Project:** PromoLeak Radar  
**Goal:** Build a shared picture of current promo/referral mechanics, pain points, and constraints before locking requirements.

## Techniques (planned mix)

| Technique | Topic area | Participants (typical) | Output |
|-----------|------------|-------------------------|--------|
| Structured interviews | Finance margin model, known leak hypotheses | FP&A, Controller delegate | Notes, quotes, data requests |
| Process observation / walkthrough | Checkout, manual overrides | Product, CS lead | As-is steps, exception list |
| Facilitated workshop | Rule ownership, stacking policy | Growth, Legal, Engineering | Decision log |
| Document review | Existing promo T&Cs, internal runbooks | Legal, Growth | Marked-up references |
| Data sampling (if permitted) | Example abuse patterns (anonymized) | Data, Fraud | Example scenarios for rules |

## Sequence (rough)

1. Finance + Data: what numbers exist today, what is trusted, what is disputed.  
2. Growth: how campaigns are built, who can publish codes, emergency shutoffs.  
3. Engineering: where rules execute (cart vs. OMS vs. payment), known limitations.  
4. CS: top ticket drivers related to discounts and referrals.  
5. Consolidation: assumptions-constraints-risks doc updated after each wave.

## Logistics

- Sessions 45–60 minutes; send questions 24h in advance (see `interview-questions.md`).
- Record only if policy allows; otherwise two note-takers for critical interviews.

## Risks to elicitation

- **Defensive metrics:** teams may downplay leakage without audit-friendly examples — anonymized samples help.
- **Tribal knowledge:** key engineers only available near release windows — schedule early.

## Status

This plan is a placeholder until calendars and named interviewees are confirmed.
